# What's new in 3.2.0

- Restart apps: press / to see which apps are still on the old theme, a to
  review them, then Ctrl+Enter to restart. They come back on their own
  workspaces.
- Auto-prompt (optional): the restart list opens by itself after a theme
  change. Nothing closes until you press Ctrl+Enter.
- Settings (s): turn the auto-prompt on or off, and choose which frameworks
  the panel shows.
- Help (?): report a problem or ask a question.
- Readable KDE icons: Okular and other KDE apps now show clear icons on dark
  themes.

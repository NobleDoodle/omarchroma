# What's new in 3.2.0

- Restart apps after a theme change. Press / for the apps still on the old
  theme, then Restart All (a). Nothing restarts until you have seen the list
  and pressed Ctrl+Enter.
- Optional: have that list come up by itself after a theme change.
- Restarted apps come back to the workspaces they were on, out of sight until
  they are there. Apps busy downloading or copying files are left open.
- Settings (s): restart after a theme change, and which optional frameworks
  the panel shows.
- Help (?) opens the issues page, to report a problem or ask a question.
- KDE apps such as Okular show readable icons on dark themes.
- Apps are listed by their own names. VS Code is listed for restart; Steam,
  which no theme reaches, never is.
